    In a real environment

    Data sources
    • Standard Lead fields such as Country, State, Lead Source and Rating. A region is not a standard field, so derive it from country and state or add a custom field.
    • Lead Owner, which can be a user or a queue, and a count of open leads per user from a report or a scheduled count.
    • Rep availability from Omni-Channel presence or a custom out-of-office field on the user. Confirm which one your org uses.
    Limits and prerequisites
    • Lead assignment rules check entries in order, the first match wins, and each entry assigns a user or a queue. Only one rule is active at a time, and a final entry with no criteria can catch the rest.
    • There is no built-in round robin rule type. The documented pattern uses an autonumber field, a formula and one entry per user.
    • Capacity-aware routing uses Omni-Channel: a flow that ends in a Route Work action, called from a record-triggered flow for leads. A service channel for the Lead object and the licensing are things to confirm in the target org.
    • Search, list view filters, sorting and row selection are ordinary list view behavior. The Routing result column, the decision trace and the side panel are illustrations: a real build would use a custom field set by the flow, a stored reason, and a report or a Lightning page component.
    • Sales Territories can also link leads, but Salesforce guidance favors standard lead assignment rules or a separate process for lead routing. Territory here is only a region field.
    How it would be validated
    • Replay a sample of past leads through the proposed rules in a sandbox and compare the owner each rule picks with the actual owner.
    • Report on leads still owned by a queue, and on leads whose owner was out or over the limit when the lead arrived.
    • Pilot one region first and review the leads that fell through before widening the rule.

    What this demo does not claim: it shows no response times, conversion or fairness results, the six leads and three reps are fictional, and the tie-break choices (first match, most room first) are illustrative. The counts shown are simple tallies of this simulation, not benchmarks. The capacity rules model what an Omni-Channel flow could do and are not a copy of a specific configuration. The interface styling is generic and is not the product itself.
